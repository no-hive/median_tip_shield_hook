## Overview

**MPF Hook** (Median Tip Shield Hook) is a Uniswap v4 hook that protects liquidity providers from MEV extraction by using median-based tip analysis to detect and shield against sandwich attacks and price manipulation.

## How It Works

The hook intercepts swap operations on Uniswap v4 pools and evaluates transaction tips against a rolling median. Transactions that deviate suspiciously from the median are flagged as likely MEV activity and can be blocked or repriced to protect LPs.
