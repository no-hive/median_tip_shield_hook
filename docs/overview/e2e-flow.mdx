This page describes the end-to-end flow executed when a swap reaches PFPHook.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Transaction Arrives at the Hook">
    On a pool call, such as a swap, the Hook reads the transaction's priority fee before execution.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare Against the Median">
    The priority fee is checked against a running median maintained by a Frugal-2U streaming-quantile estimator.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compute the Swap Fee">
    The swap fee is scaled proportionally to the amount by which the priority fee exceeds the median. The larger the excess, the higher the fee applied to the swap.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Distribute the Fee">
    All collected fees are routed to liquidity providers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the Median">
    After the swap fee is charged, the transaction's priority fee is fed back into the estimator, updating the median for subsequent transactions.
  </Step>
</Steps>
