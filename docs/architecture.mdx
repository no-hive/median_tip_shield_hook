## Overview

Every swap that touches an MPF-enabled pool passes through a five-step pipeline. The hook reads the transaction's priority fee, compares it to a running median, charges a proportional penalty, and feeds the observation back into the estimator.

## Transaction flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Transaction arrives at the hook">
    On a pool call (swap), the hook reads the transaction's priority fee before execution.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare against the median">
    The priority fee is checked against a running median maintained by a Frugal-2U (streaming quantile) estimator.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compute the swap fee">
    The swap fee is scaled proportionally to how far the priority fee exceeds the median. The larger the excess, the higher the fee applied to the swap.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Distribute the fee">
    All collected fees are routed to liquidity providers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the median">
    After the swap fee is charged, the transaction's priority fee is fed back into the estimator, correcting the median for subsequent transactions.
  </Step>
</Steps>
