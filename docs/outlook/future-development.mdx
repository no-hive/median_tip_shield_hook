## Further Mathematical Refinement

The mathematical configuration can be refined further. This documentation intentionally presents multiple approaches and comparisons because the currently selected methods and curves may not be optimal in every deployment context.

Further experiments can evaluate more precise models, alternative curves, and parameter configurations. Developers can use the mathematical materials to adapt the Hook's calculations to the characteristics and protection requirements of their own pools.

## Launchpad Integrations

PFPHook is designed to operate independently of external modules. Combined with its accessibility for smaller pools, this makes it a potentially strong fit for launchpads operating on emerging or less established networks, where external infrastructure may be limited.

A primary future direction is integration with launchpads of different sizes. Its self-contained design can help launchpad teams deploy pools with similar protection properties without relying on additional external services.

PFPHook is distributed under the MIT License. Its open and non-commercial licensing model can be particularly useful for launchpads built by small teams.

## Independent Security Audit

An independent security audit would be valuable for PFPHook. Although the project has undergone an internal review, external assessment is especially important because the Hook includes casts and calculation curves with non-trivial mathematical behavior.

An external audit can provide an additional review of the implementation, mathematical assumptions, edge cases, and potential security risks before broader adoption.
